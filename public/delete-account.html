Account & Data Deletion

Application: CubedPDF (com.aistudio.pdftools.xqmztb) • Developer Contact: joydigitalworld2023@gmail.com

Permanent Action: Deleting your CubedPDF account permanently removes your Firebase Authentication account, your profile preferences, and your synced document history from Cloud Firestore. Your local PDF files stored on your Android device are never deleted by cloud account removal.

Option 1: Delete Instantly Inside the CubedPDF App (Recommended)

  1. Open the CubedPDF Android app and make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap your Account Profile icon in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the Profile panel and tap Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  4. Confirm deletion. For your security, if your last sign-in was a while ago, CubedPDF will ask you to quickly re-verify with Google Sign-In or your password, then immediately erase your Cloud Firestore records and Firebase Authentication account.

Option 2: Request Account or Data Deletion Online (Without Reinstalling the App)

If you have already uninstalled CubedPDF or prefer to request deletion from the web, complete the form below or email joydigitalworld2023@gmail.com directly from your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Action Upon Deletion Timeline / Retention
Firebase Authentication Account (UID, email, sign-in credentials) Permanently deleted Immediate (in-app) or within 7 business days (web request)
Cloud Firestore Profile (users/{uid}: name, workspace role) Permanently deleted Immediate (in-app) or within 7 business days (web request)
Synced Document History Metadata (users/{uid}/recent_docs) Permanently deleted Immediate (in-app) or within 7 business days (web request)
Local PDF Files & Device Room Database Stored strictly on your device; never uploaded to our servers Controlled solely by you on your device (cleared if you clear app storage or uninstall)
Automated Infrastructure Backups & Aggregate Analytics Non-identifying aggregate logs / rolling backups purged automatically Up to 30 days standard cloud backup rotation; never restored to active user accounts
